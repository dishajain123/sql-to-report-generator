## Purpose Summary

This procedure determines and updates the Special Mention Account (SMA) classification for banking accounts based on their overdue and operational status, in line with regulatory and internal asset quality norms. It ensures that accounts are correctly marked for early warning (SMA-0, SMA-1, SMA-2) or other asset classes, records the reasons for such classifications, and maintains historical movement records for both accounts and customers. The process also resets or updates related operational fields to maintain data integrity and compliance with reporting standards.

## Step-by-Step Logic Flow

1. Retrieve the process date and effective period for the current run based on the provided time key.
2. Identify accounts under specific schemes (e.g., Aqua Scheme with ODA type and CC/OD facility) that are eligible for SMA evaluation.
3. For eligible accounts, reset continuous excess and overdue days fields to zero if the overdrawn days are within the permissible limit.
4. Prepare a working set of accounts with significant overdue or overdrawn days for further SMA assessment.
5. Ensure all Days Past Due (DPD) metrics are non-negative by resetting any negative values to zero.
6. For each account, calculate the maximum DPD value across all relevant DPD metrics (interest not serviced, no credit, overdrawn, overdue, renewal, stock statement).
7. Clear previous SMA classification fields before recalculating.
8. Assign the SMA classification (SMA_0, SMA_1, SMA_2) to each account based on the maximum DPD value, and record the reason for the classification.
9. Set the SMA effective date and flag the account as SMA-marked.
10. Aggregate SMA classifications at the customer and UCIF (unique customer identifier) levels for consolidated reporting.
11. Update customer-level SMA keys and dates based on the most severe account-level SMA status.
12. Maintain movement history for both accounts and customers, recording changes in SMA status and asset class.
13. Update descriptive fields for customer movement based on asset class and SMA status for reporting.
14. On completion or error, update the process status and clean up temporary data structures.

# Business Conditions Report — SMA_MARKING

> **What this process does:** This procedure determines and updates the Special Mention Account (SMA) classification for banking accounts based on their overdue and operational status, in line with regulatory and internal asset quality norms. It ensures that accounts are correctly marked for early warning (SMA-0, SMA-1, SMA-2) or other asset classes, records the reasons for such classifications, and maintains historical movement records for both accounts and customers. The process also resets or updates related operational fields to maintain data integrity and compliance with reporting standards.

## Calculations / Formulas

- **Process date for SMA marking:** The process date is determined by looking up the date in the SYSDAYMATRIX table for the given time key.
- **Effective to time key for movement history:** The effective to time key is set to one less than the time key from the Automate_Advances table where the extension flag is 'Y'.
- **Conditional DPD metrics:** Each DPD metric is set to its value if it is greater than or equal to the reference period value, otherwise it is set to zero.
- **Maximum DPD value (DPD_Max):** The maximum DPD value is calculated as the highest value among all DPD metrics for the account.
- **SMA effective date:** The SMA effective date is calculated as the process date minus the maximum DPD plus one day.
- **Customer-level maximum SMA class:** The customer-level SMA class key is set to the highest numeric value among the customer's account SMA classes.
- **Customer-level earliest SMA date:** The customer-level SMA date is set to the earliest SMA date among the customer's accounts.
